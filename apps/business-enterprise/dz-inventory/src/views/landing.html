GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you start.

Is Inventory free to use?

Yes. Inventory is free to use and has no ads. A Puter account is required to save and sync your inventory.

Can I try it with sample data?

Yes. Sign in and choose “Explore with sample data” in the welcome screen. The sample banner lets you clear that dataset when you are ready to start your own inventory.

Can I bring an existing spreadsheet?

Export it as CSV, then review the column mapping and SKU matches in Inventory. You can update products, add to stock, or skip matches. See the import walkthrough.

Can I track more than one location?

Yes. Each product can hold stock, a shelf code, and a minimum at each location. Transfers move units between places without changing the total quantity.

Does it work with a barcode scanner?

Use a compatible USB or Bluetooth scanner that types a barcode, enter codes yourself, or try the camera scanner where supported. Camera scanning requires browser support and camera permission.

Can I keep working offline?

After loading the app online, its cached app shell and local mirror support connection drops. Changes queue on this device until you reconnect. A first visit or a new sign-in still needs a connection. Check the sync status before switching devices.

How do I take my data with me?

Settings includes product CSV export and a full JSON backup. Use the backup to restore records, including history and settings. Restoring replaces the current dataset; review the backup workflow first.
