### 🎯 Overview
Alternative productivity suite built to replace heavy proprietary office software, with out-of-the-box templates for official Algerian administrative forms, certificates, and reports.

---

### 📋 Planned Projects & Specifications

| Program ID | Algerian Target Application | Proprietary Alternative Replaced | Key Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`dz-docs`** | **DZ-Docs (محرر المستندات والنصوص)** | Microsoft Word | Native bi-directional mixed Arabic-French formatting without cursor jumping, pre-installed templates for official Algerian ministries, certificates, and legal memos. |
| **`dz-sheets`** | **DZ-Sheets (الجداول الحسابية)** | Microsoft Excel | High-performance calculation grid, built-in Algerian financial functions (`=TAFQIT_AR()`, `=TVA_DZ()`, `=TIMBRE_DZ()`), CSV/XLSX export. |
| **`dz-pdf`** | **DZ-PDF Suite (أدوات ملفات PDF)** | Adobe Acrobat Pro, iLovePDF | 100% offline PDF merger, page splitter, document compressor, digital signature stamp, and form field filling for administrative S12 & legal certificates. |
| **`dz-slides`** | **DZ-Present (العروض التقديمية)** | Microsoft PowerPoint | Minimalist presentation builder tailored for Algerian university defenses (PFE / Soutenance) and school classrooms. |
