# Notes

**Your digital notebook, stored in your own cloud**

## Short description

A free-form notebook for everything you want to remember. Write anywhere on the page, organize with notebooks, sections and pages, tag to-dos, draw by hand and find anything in seconds.

## Long description

Notes works like a paper notebook that never runs out of pages. Click anywhere on a page and start typing: every note sits in its own container you can move and resize, so lists, tables, pictures and sketches can live side by side the way you think about them.

Organize with notebooks, colored sections, section groups and pages with subpages. Drag pages and sections into the order you want, or move and copy them between notebooks. Tag paragraphs as To Do, Important, Question and more, then open the tag summary to see every tagged note across your notebooks and check off what is done.

Pick up a pen or highlighter on the Draw tab to handwrite or sketch, lasso ink to move it, and insert shapes. Add tables, files, pictures, screenshots, equations, links to other pages, and audio or video recordings. Start pages from templates for meetings, to-do lists, lectures, projects and journals.

Search every notebook at once, read pages in a distraction-free reading view with read-aloud, go back to earlier page versions, and restore anything you delete for 60 days. Share a page as a read-only link, export it as a web page, Markdown or PDF, or back up a whole notebook in one file.

Your notebooks are stored as files in your own Puter cloud, not in a shared database, and are never sold or shown to advertisers. Notes works in any modern browser on a laptop, tablet or phone, and follows your light or dark system setting.

## Key features

- Free-form pages: click anywhere to write, then move and resize note containers.
- Notebooks, colored sections, section groups, pages and subpages with drag and drop.
- Tags with keyboard shortcuts and a Find Tags summary across notebooks.
- Pens, highlighters, eraser, lasso select, shapes and insert space for ink.
- Tables, pictures, attachments, screenshots, equations, links, recordings and templates.
- Search across notebooks, find on page, and recent notes.
- Immersive Reader with adjustable text and read-aloud.
- Undo, page versions and Deleted Notes.
- Read-only share links, export to HTML, Markdown, text and PDF, and notebook backups.
- AI Assist to summarize pages and find action items, plus dictation.
- Light, dark and system themes; full keyboard support.

## Who it's for

- Professionals keeping meeting notes and action items
- Project teams planning and tracking work
- Writers and researchers collecting ideas, sources and clippings
- Anyone who wants a private notebook in an account they control
