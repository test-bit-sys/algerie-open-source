# Wiki

**Docs, notes and databases for you and your team**

## Short description

Write with blocks, organize pages in a tree, track work in databases and share pages with your team. Stored in your own Puter cloud.

## Long description

Wiki is a home for everything you write and track. Start typing on any page and use / to add headings, lists, to-dos, toggles, quotes, callouts, code, tables, images and links to other pages. Markdown shortcuts work as you type, and every block can be dragged into place.

Nest pages as deep as you like, give them icons and covers, star the ones you use most and pick up where you left off from Home. Templates get meeting notes, project briefs, weekly plans, task boards and reading lists started in a click.

Databases turn pages into rows. Track tasks on a board, plan on a calendar, browse a gallery or edit a table, with properties for status, dates, tags, numbers, checkboxes and links, and filters and sorts for every view.

Share a page and everything under it with the people you choose. They can view, comment or edit, you see who is on the same page, and everyone's changes merge as they type. Comment on a whole page or on one block, reply and resolve.

Private pages are stored as files in your own Puter cloud, not in a shared database, and they are never sold or shown to advertisers. Shared pages stay in the account of the person who shared them. Wiki works on a laptop, tablet or phone, follows your light or dark setting and is built to be usable with a keyboard and a screen reader.

## Key features

- Block editor with a / menu, Markdown shortcuts, drag and drop and page links.
- Nested pages with icons, covers, favorites, templates and a trash.
- Databases with table, board, list, gallery and calendar views.
- Quick search across every page.
- Sharing with view, comment and edit roles, and live editing together.
- Comments on pages and blocks.
- Markdown import, Markdown and HTML export, print to PDF.
- System, light and dark themes.
