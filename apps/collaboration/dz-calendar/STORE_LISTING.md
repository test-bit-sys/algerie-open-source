# Calendar

**Your calendar, your data.**

## Short description

Month, week, day and list views with recurring events, reminders, guests and iCalendar import and export. Your events stay in your own storage.

## Long description

Calendar gives you a complete, fast calendar in the browser. Six views cover every way you plan: a full year at a glance, a month grid, a week or four day timeline, a single focused day, and a grouped list of everything coming up. Overlapping meetings sit side by side, a live line tracks the current time, and you can drag any event to reschedule it or drag its edge to change the length.

Everything you need for real scheduling is built in. Set an event to repeat daily, weekly on the weekdays you pick, monthly by date or by weekday, yearly, or on a custom interval, then edit or cancel a single occurrence, everything from a point forward, or the whole series. Add reminders that raise a browser notification before an occurrence, invite guests and collect their answers, block out time as out of office, and keep to-dos beside your meetings so your day is in one place.

Your Puter mailbox can feed it directly. Connect it once and an invitation emailed to your address is read where it arrives and put on the calendar, even while the app is closed. Invitations from people you already know are added for you, or wait in a list where you can add, ignore, or block the sender when the message cannot be verified; mail from a stranger stays out of sight unless you ask to see it, so nothing lands on your calendar because someone decided to email you. Answering sends a real reply their calendar understands.

What makes Calendar different is where your data lives. It signs in with a free Puter account and writes every event to your own Puter storage, so there is no separate account to create and no shared database holding your schedule. You can export the whole calendar to an iCalendar or JSON file at any time and import it somewhere else. Recurring series survive the round trip, including individual occurrences you changed or cancelled.

It works the way you expect on every screen. On a phone you get a bottom tab bar and a slide out sidebar; on a desktop you get the full three pane layout. Install it to your home screen or dock, and a previously loaded calendar still opens when you are offline.

## Key features

- **Switch between six views**, year, month, week, four day, day and list, each tuned to the screen it runs on.
- **Repeat anything**, daily, weekly on chosen weekdays, monthly by date or weekday, yearly or a custom interval, then change one occurrence, this and following, or the entire series.
- **Drag to reschedule**, move an event across the grid or pull its edge to resize it, snapped to quarter hours, with overlapping events laid out side by side.
- **Get reminded**, attach one or more notifications to an event and receive a browser alert ahead of each occurrence.
- **Invite guests and track replies**, send an RSVP link, see who is in, out or undecided, and answer invitations with one tap.
- **Connect your Puter mailbox**, and invitations from people you know appear on the calendar by themselves. Strangers stay hidden unless you ask to see them, and you can answer any invitation by email in one press.
- **Find any event instantly**, open the search palette with a keystroke and jump straight to a match by title, location or description.
- **Own your data**, sign in with a free Puter account, keep events in your own storage, and export to iCalendar or JSON whenever you want.
- **Keep planning offline**, install it as an app and open your calendar without a connection.

## Who it's for

- Individuals who want a clean, quick daily calendar without signing up for another service.
- Small teams coordinating recurring meetings and tracking who has replied.
- Freelancers and consultants juggling client calls, deadlines and personal time in one place.
- Anyone moving off a heavier calendar who needs iCalendar import and export to bring history along.
- Privacy minded users who would rather keep their schedule in storage they control.

## Highlights in this version

- Guest lists with shareable RSVP links, so people can reply without an account of their own.
- Tasks with due dates, shown alongside events in every view and checked off in place.
