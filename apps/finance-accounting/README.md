# Finance & Accounting (برامج المالية والمحاسبة)
## DZ Finance — Fiscal, Accounting & Banking Systems Track

[English](README.md) • [العربية](../../README.ar.md) • [Français](../../README.fr.md)

---

### Mission
Provide Algerian chartered accountants, auditors, and finance officers with audit-ready, legal-compliant financial accounting and payroll tools.

---

### Planned Project Blueprints

| Project Identifier | Target Application | Proprietary Equivalent | Algerian Specifics & Key Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`dz-compta`** | **DZ-Compta SCF** | PC Compta, DLG Compta | Full adherence to Algerian Financial Accounting System (Système Comptable Financier - SCF), General Ledger (Grand Livre), Balance Sheet (Bilan), TCR, Journal Centralisateur. |
| **`dz-g50`** | **DZ-Fiscal Assistant (G50)** | Manual Excel tax filing | Automated generation and error-checking for monthly tax declarations (G50: TVA, TAP, IRG Salaires, IBS acomptes). |
| **`dz-paie`** | **DZ-Paie & RH** | PC Paie | Salary slips (Fiches de paie) according to Algerian labor law, updated IRG scale brackets, CNAS social declarations (9% worker, 26% employer), ATS forms. |
| **`dz-banking`** | **DZ-Treasury & Postal Slip** | Manual checkbooks | Automated print generation for Algérie Poste CCP checks, postal money transfers, bank remittance slips, and RIB/RIP validation. |
